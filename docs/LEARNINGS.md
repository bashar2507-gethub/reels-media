# Learnings from our own numbers

A dated entry every 5 published Reels (RUNBOOK step 1b). Groups of fewer than 3 Reels are hints, not results.
Numbers come from `content/analytics/summary.json` (Instagram, via Metricool).

## 2026-11-15 — baseline, 12 Reels (poc1 to r011)

- Overall: 66% skip in the first 3 s, 6.8 s average watch (18% of the video), 2.7% likes per reach,
  0.4% shares per reach, 0 comments.
- By category: muscle 60% skip and 8.6 s watch (n=6); fat_loss 71% and 5.0 s (n=6).
- By hook type: how_many 55% and 9.7 s (n=2); situation 60% and 10.2 s (n=1); yes_no 66% and 6.5 s (n=6);
  versus 68% and 4.9 s (n=1); why 77% and 4.1 s (n=2).
- By intro: none 64% and 7.5 s (n=8); intro at the start 69% and 5.5 s (n=4, all fat-loss topics).
- Best: r006 weekly sets (47% skip, 10.9 s). Worst: r003 ab exercises (80% skip) and r001 weight plateau (3.3 s).
- Adjustments for the next five Reels: training topics first (plan reordered: r014, r015, r013, r016, r017, ...),
  and prefer how_many, situation and versus hooks over why hooks. Proposed to the owner: hook-first intro.
