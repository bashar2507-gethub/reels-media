## The brief

BLANKET UNIVERSE – CHARACTER BRIEF

This document defines the two main characters of the Blanket universe. These character profiles are the foundation
for future videos, animations, storytelling, visual interactions, and comedy. They can be refined and expanded later.

### 1. BLANKET – THE MALE CHARACTER

Personality: Serious, calm, practical, rational, disciplined, and analytical.

Blanket is the established main character, already introduced to the audience through previous videos. He has a
composed personality and approaches situations with logic, facts, and clear reasoning.

* Calm and emotionally controlled.
* Serious and professional.
* Practical, intelligent, and analytical.
* Confident without being arrogant.
* Direct and focused.
* Rarely overreacts.
* Prefers facts over assumptions.
* His humor comes naturally from his seriousness, dry reactions, and maintaining composure in absurd situations.

Role: The rational voice of the universe. He represents knowledge, science, fitness, discipline, and practical
thinking.

Behavior: Minimal, deliberate movements. Subtle expressions. Controlled reactions. He should never appear overly
energetic, childish, or exaggerated.

### 2. MARAH – THE FEMALE CHARACTER

Personality: Cheerful, playful, spontaneous, curious, lovable, expressive, and unintentionally funny.

Marah is a new female character introduced into the Blanket universe. She is intelligent and confident, but her
spontaneous nature, occasional naivety, overconfidence, and unexpected reactions create humorous situations.

* Energetic and playful.
* Curious and adventurous.
* Confident, sometimes without realizing she is wrong.
* Innocent and occasionally naive.
* Emotionally expressive.
* Can misunderstand simple situations or take things too literally.
* Sometimes acts before thinking.
* Naturally funny without intentionally trying to be a comedian.
* Has a sweet personality but is not weak, shy, or childish.

Role: The entertaining and relatable side of the universe. She represents curiosity, human behavior, everyday
struggles, reactions, and the fun side of fitness and self-improvement.

Behavior: More expressive body language, dynamic movements, playful reactions, curiosity, excitement, and occasional
exaggerated gestures. Her humor should feel natural rather than forced.

### 3. CHARACTER DYNAMIC

Blanket and Marah are opposites, and their contrast is the foundation of their chemistry.

* Blanket represents logic, discipline, and control.
* Marah represents spontaneity, curiosity, and emotion.
* Blanket thinks before acting.
* Marah often acts before thinking.
* Blanket explains things seriously.
* Marah reacts in unexpected ways.
* Blanket maintains composure.
* Marah creates unpredictable situations.

Their comedy should emerge naturally from their contrasting personalities, reactions, misunderstandings, and
interactions.

Both characters must remain consistent across all videos, regardless of the subject, setting, animation style, or
scenario.

Important: These are personality briefs only. Their designs, dialogue styles, voices, backstories, relationships, and
additional character traits can be developed or modified later.
